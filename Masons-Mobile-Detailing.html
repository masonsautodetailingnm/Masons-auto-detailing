Questions

Good to know

Do you really come to me?
Yes — every detail is mobile. We bring all the professional detailing equipment and product with us to your home, office, or wherever the vehicle is parked within 30 miles of Norman, OK. All we need from you is access to water and electricity.
How fast can I get on the schedule?
Most requests are confirmed within a few hours, and we can usually get you booked within 1–3 days. Call or text for same-week openings.
How long does a detail take?
Exterior details run about an hour, interiors about 90 minutes, and a full detail around 2.5 hours. Ceramic coatings are a full-day service.
What does pricing depend on?
Vehicle size and condition. Listed prices are starting points for a coupe/sedan — we always confirm the final number before any work begins.
